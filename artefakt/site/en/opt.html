01 / Process

Four steps
from intake
to the box.

One water, one workshop, one process.

River fish comes only from the Tsimlyansk reservoir. Every batch goes through four steps and leaves for the warehouse in a GOST-92 corrugated box.

Order of work
Step 01

Intake

Tsimlyansk fish from the local fishery.

River fish is Tsimlyansk only. Before salting, it's frozen solid: the standard only calls for −18 °C, our freezer holds −20 °C. Frozen this way, bream keeps for up to six months.

Step 02

Salting

An old recipe, salted in the cellar.

Salted in the cellar, never above +8 °C. Salt is packed into the gills, then the fish is laid in a container and covered in salt.

Step 03

Curing

A climate-controlled drying room since 2007.

Computers watch the whole process. A batch hangs for exactly two days — not a day and a half, not three. Production depends on neither weather nor season.

Step 04

Packaging

GOST-92 corrugated box, 10 kg.

Cold-smoked vimba, sabrefish, herring and mackerel go in GOST-105 boxes, 5 kg. Lightly salted fish ships in an 11 L bucket, 8 kg net. A full set of paperwork leaves with every batch.
