Two concepts. Two full sites.

Two links below. Two design concepts for the new site — «Rosso» and «Artefakt», each a full six-page site: home, history, shop, wholesale, price list, delivery. View them in any order; come back here with the browser's «back» button.

01 Rosso Film noir

The strict grid of automotive design-system language, carried over into film noir: steps of dark surfaces, all-caps microtypography, zero radii, zero shadows. One red accent for the whole site, and a white technical dossier under the price list — the only light break in the black.
